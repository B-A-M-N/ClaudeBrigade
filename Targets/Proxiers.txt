ByteCatCode & CrowLLM — Infrastructure & Security Analysis
=============================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
Both services are NewAPI (formerly OneAPI) deployments — the same open-source
AI gateway software. They share identical frontend build fingerprints, near-
identical configuration, and sequential deployment timing. They are almost
certainly operated by the same actor.

DO NOT USE AS UPSTREAM PROVIDERS for any production gateway. They are:
- Key harvesters (collect all upstream provider credentials)
- Traffic interceptors (log all prompts/responses)
- Chinese-jurisdiction services (explicit "move to police" threats)
- Operationally unstable (broken auth, 502 monitoring, nginx timeouts)
